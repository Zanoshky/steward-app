Runbook

When something goes wrong

What you see, why it happens, and the taps that fix it. Most fixes start at the top of Settings: when Android holds something back, a Needs a fix card shows it with a Fix button. More answers are in the questions.

Alerts do not arrive

Notifications are off, an alert switch is off, the subscription has ended, or the news was already known when you added the flight.

  1. Settings: if a Needs a fix card shows Notifications, tap Fix and allow them.
  2. In Android's notification settings for Steward, check that Gate, delay and cancellation, Take off, landing and bags, and Check in, leave and boarding are not off or silent.
  3. Settings, Alerts: check the switch for that kind of alert.
  4. Settings, Your plan. Without a live subscription Steward gets no alerts at all, cancellations included.
  5. A gate already given when you added the flight is not sent again; only a change is. A second alert of the same kind replaces the first.

The live update is not on the lock screen

It starts two hours before departure, for one flight at a time. It may be switched off, unpinned for that flight, or not allowed to sit on the lock screen.

  1. Settings, Alerts: turn on Live on the lock screen.
  2. On Android 16 and newer, Settings, Needs a fix: if it says Live updates are off for Steward, tap Fix and allow it.
  3. If you unpinned or swiped it away, open the flight and tap Show the live update again.
  4. Check that the phone's lock screen shows notifications.

Leave now or boarding came a few minutes late

Exact timing is off, so Android may hold the reminder back while the phone sleeps.

  1. Settings: in the Needs a fix card, tap Fix on Exact timing.
  2. Allow Alarms and reminders for Steward, then come back.

The pass asks me to unlock first

Show on the lock screen is off.

  1. Settings, Boarding pass, turn on Show on the lock screen.

The calendar finds nothing

Steward reads only flights that Gmail put into Google Calendar, from today on, and only with your OK.

  1. Settings, Calendar, turn on Look in my calendar, and allow the calendar.
  2. In Google Calendar's settings, turn on Events from Gmail.
  3. If Android no longer asks, Steward opens its app settings: allow Calendar there.
  4. Or add the flight by its number.

The calendar found the wrong flight

The flight number or day in the event did not match your trip.

  1. Tap Not mine to hide it. It stays hidden for good.
  2. Add the right flight by its number with the local departure day.
  3. To stop calendar finds, Settings, Calendar, turn off Look in my calendar.

A boarding pass will not read

Steward could not find the barcode, or the pass is for another flight.

  1. For a picture, use a sharper screenshot of the barcode.
  2. If it says the pass is for another flight, add that flight first, or open the right pass.
  3. If the gate scanner cannot read Steward's barcode, use the airline's own pass.

My trips say they are not watched

The subscription ended or has not started. Your trips stay to read, but nothing is watched.

  1. Settings, Your plan, See plans, and subscribe again. Steward picks up where it left off.

I paid but Steward is still locked

The purchase is on another Google account, the payment is pending, or the phone has not asked Google Play yet.

  1. Open the Play Store and check you are signed in with the account that bought the plan.
  2. Settings, Your plan, Restore purchase.
  3. If it says your payment is pending, Steward turns on when Google Play clears it.

Restore purchase says no subscription

The Google account on this phone has no Steward plan. A plan bought with another account restores from that account.

  1. Switch the Play Store to the account that bought it, then Restore purchase again.

It says this month's flights are used

Steward follows 10 new flights a month, Pro 30, Unlimited with no count. Connections on the same trip, and flights another of your phones already follows, are free.

  1. Tap 10 more flights for 1.99; pack flights never run out.
  2. Or tap See plans and move up a plan.
  3. Or wait for the 1st, when the count starts again. A flight you removed within an hour was already given back.

The subscription will not start

Google Play is not set up on this phone, not reachable, or the phone is offline.

  1. Open the Play Store once and check you are signed in.
  2. Update the Play Store, get online, then try again.

Cancel the subscription or the trial

  1. Settings, Your plan, Manage subscription. Or Play Store, your profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Steward.
  2. Tap Cancel subscription. Cancel a trial before the 3 days end and you are not charged.

Get a refund

Google Play takes the payment, so refunds follow Google Play's rules.

  1. Ask Google Play: open your order history at play.google.com, find the Steward order, and request a refund.
  2. If Google sends you to the developer, write to support@zanoski.com with the order number.
  3. A refund ends the plan. Cancelling does not refund; it keeps Steward on to the end of the time you paid for.

There is no photo of my plane

Not every plane has a photo on Planespotters.net, and a plane shows only once the airline has named it, about a day before the flight.

  1. If it says No photo of the plane's registration yet, tap Look on Planespotters.net to search for it there.
  2. If it says Could not reach the photos, get online and try again in a moment.

The widget is out of date

The widget redraws on alerts, syncs and the system's widget period, so it can lag while the phone sleeps.

  1. Open Steward once.
  2. Still wrong? Remove the widget and add it again.

My access code does not work

Codes come from Steward support, for example for a review or after a problem. Each code works for a set number of phones and days.

  1. Settings, Your plan, Access code. Type the code with or without the dashes; letters can be small or capital.
  2. "That code is not right": check each letter. Codes never use 0, O, 1, I or L.
  3. "Used up or has ended": write to support@zanoski.com for a new one.
  4. "Already has a subscription": this phone pays already, so it needs no code.

A red note says No connection

The phone is offline, or Steward cannot reach its server. What it last heard stays on screen, with the time it heard it.

  1. Check Wi-Fi or mobile data. Airport Wi-Fi often needs its sign in page first: open a browser.
  2. Roaming off abroad: turn on roaming for data, or join Wi-Fi.
  3. The note goes by itself once Steward hears from its server. Until then, check the airport screens for the gate.

Delete my data

Steward has no account; what its server keeps belongs to the phone the app is on.

  1. Settings, Promises, permissions and your data, Download my data first if you want a copy.
  2. Settings, Promises, permissions and your data, Delete my data, Delete.
  3. Already uninstalled? Read steward.zanoski.com/delete, or write to support@zanoski.com with the number and day of a flight you followed.
  4. Deleting data does not cancel the subscription. Cancel it in Google Play.
