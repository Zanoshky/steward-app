Cookbook

Short recipes

A few taps each. Everything here needs a live subscription, except the plan and data recipes.

Add a flight by its number

  1. Tap the round add button in the bar.
  2. Pick the day from the chips above the box, or Later for another day.
  3. Type the flight number, like OU 410, and search.
  4. Tap the flight to follow it.

Add the flights in your calendar

  1. Settings, Calendar, turn on Look in my calendar, and allow the calendar.
  2. Open Flights. Under Found in your calendar, tap Add, or Not mine.

Add a trip from the booking email

  1. Open the booking email in Gmail or the trip in the airline app.
  2. Tap Share and pick Steward.
  3. Steward lists the flights it found. Add the ones you take.

Add a boarding pass

  1. Open the flight, then Pass.
  2. Pick A screenshot, or A PDF or Wallet pass.
  3. The pass is kept with that flight, with its seat.

Have the pass open over the lock screen

  1. Settings, Boarding pass, turn on Show on the lock screen.
  2. From boarding time, tap the live update on the lock screen. Only the pass shows.

Put your boarding pass in Quick Settings

  1. Keep your first boarding pass. On Android 13 and newer, Steward offers the tile once: tap Add.
  2. Missed it? Edit your Quick Settings tiles and drag in Boarding pass. It shows your next pass, one tap away.

Share your ETA

  1. Open the flight and tap Share.
  2. Pick the app to send it with, or Copy live link. The chat shows a card of the flight, and the page follows it live in the reader's own time.
  3. To end it early: the flight, then Stop sharing the live link.

Know when to leave for the airport

  1. Settings, Leaving for the airport, set Time to the airport and Be at the airport.
  2. For a guess from where you are on the day, turn on From where I am. The longer time wins.

Switch to miles or Fahrenheit

  1. Settings, Units.
  2. Tap °C or °F for Temperature, and km or mi for Distance. Every distance in the app follows it.

Follow a connection

  1. Add every leg of the trip, or share the booking or boarding pass so Steward adds them all.
  2. Open the first flight. The Connecting flight card shows the next leg and how much time you have.
  3. Tap the card to open the next leg. Back takes you to the leg before.
  4. After you land, Next pass opens the boarding pass for the next leg.

See how your flight usually does

  1. Open the flight and tap My plane.
  2. At the top: how late it usually leaves and lands. Below: your plane, and from 25 hours out the plane before yours.

See a photo of your plane

  1. Open the flight and tap the Plane box or the Aircraft row. Or tap My plane for the photo with the plane's age, seats and engines.
  2. The photo shows with the photographer's name. Tap the credit to open the photo page on Planespotters.net.

Keep flights in your calendar

  1. Settings, Calendar, turn on Keep flights in my calendar, and allow the calendar.
  2. Each flight is written with its latest times, terminal and gate. Turn it off and every event Steward wrote is removed.

Bring back the live update

  1. Open the flight.
  2. Tap Show the live update again.

Start your subscription

  1. Settings, Your plan, Start your subscription.
  2. Pick Steward, Pro or Unlimited, then Monthly or Yearly.
  3. Tap Try 3 days free. Google Play shows the price before you pay.

Change your plan

  1. Settings, Your plan, tap your plan (Change).
  2. Pick the plan and the period, then tap Switch to.

Cancel your subscription

  1. Settings, Your plan, Manage subscription.
  2. In Google Play, tap Cancel subscription. Steward keeps working to the end of the time you paid for.

Use your plan on another phone

  1. Install Steward on the other phone, signed in to Google Play with the account that bought the plan.
  2. Settings, Your plan, Restore purchase.

Get a copy of your data

  1. Settings, Promises, permissions and your data, Download my data.
  2. Pick where to save the file. It is a JSON file.

Delete your data

  1. Settings, Promises, permissions and your data, Delete my data.
  2. Tap Delete. Steward erases this phone from its server, removes the calendar events it wrote, clears the phone and closes.
  3. This does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it in Google Play.
